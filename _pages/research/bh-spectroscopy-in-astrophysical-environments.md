## Black-Hole Spectroscopy and Quasi-Normal Modes (QNMs)
------
There is a famous saying in the gravitational-wave (GW) community: _"Black holes are the hydrogen atom of astrophysics."_ In quantum mechanics, the stationary states of the hydrogen atom are determined by the radial part of the Schrödinger equation

$$\left[ -\frac{\hbar^2}{2m} \frac{d^2}{dr^2} + V(r) \right] \psi(r) = E \psi(r)$$

In general relativity (GR), small perturbations on a rotating black holes (BHs) are governed by the Teukolsky equation. Since the black hole is spinning, the spacetime is more complex, but the equations can still be separated into distinct radial and angular components. By switching to generalized "tortoise coordinates", ${r_* }$ defined by the relation $\frac{dr_*}{dr} = \frac{r^2+a^2}{\Delta}$, where $\Delta = r^2 - 2Mr + a^2$, the event horizon is once again stretched out to negative infinity. Through specific mathematical transformations (such as the Sasaki-Nakamura SN or Detweiler transformations), the radial part of the Teukolsky equation maps directly onto the exact same Schrödinger-like wave equation

$$\left[ -\frac{d^2}{d{r_*}^2} + V_{\text{eff}}(r, \omega, m, a) \right] \Psi({r_*}) = \omega^2 \Psi(r_*)$$

Just as the hydrogen atom served as the fundamental corner stone for quantum mechanics, black holes (BHs) are the simplest, most fundamental macroscopic objects in GR. In the early 20th century, physicists decoded the discrete emission lines of hydrogen to uncover the hidden laws of quantum physics. Today, GW astronomers are performing a remarkably similar feat by "listening" to the resonant vibrations of spacetime itself. 

When 2 BHs collide, the newly formed remnant is violently deformed. As it sheds this excess energy and settles into a stable, quiescent state, it "rings" like a struck bell. It emits GWs at specific, discrete frequencies with characteristic decay rates, known as quasi-normal modes (QNMs). Since a perfect black hole is entirely defined by just its mass, spin, and charge (thanks to the BH uniqueness theorem and no-hair conjectire) this ringdown spectrum is uniquely predetermined
